# Question
At its 2027 announcement, will the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists set the Doomsday Clock closer to midnight than 85 seconds? 

# Resolves 
By the Bulletin's official 2027 statement. YES if the setting is under [85 seconds](https://thebulletin.org/doomsday-clock/2026-statement/). Unchanged or farther from midnight is NO. Annulled if there's no announcement by Mar 31, 2027.
